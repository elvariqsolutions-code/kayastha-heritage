  • New Pen/Kalam

  • New Notebook/Bahi
  • Sandalwood Paste
  • Red Thread (Mauli)
  • Turmeric & Vermilion

Ritual Staples

Incense sticks, Ghee lamp, Betel nuts, Flowers, Seasonal fruits, and Sweets (Panchamrit).

Sacred Pooja Vidhi

01

Purification & Invocation

Clean the pooja space and place a wooden stool. Spread a red or yellow cloth and place the image or idol of Lord Chitragupta. Bathe the idol with Ganga water or Panchamrit.

02

The Pen & Ink Ritual

Place the new pen and inkwell before the Lord. Apply Tilak (Sandalwood/Vermilion) to the pen and the notebook. Offer flowers and Akshat (unbroken rice) to the stationery.

03

Account of Deeds

Write the names of family members and their earnings/expenses for the year in the notebook. This symbolizes transparency before the Lord. Pray for prosperity and wisdom.

Shri Chitragupta Katha

According to the Puranas, after Lord Brahma created the universe, he was tired. He had created the Devtas, Asuras, and Humans, but needed someone to maintain the records of their karma. He sat in meditation for eleven thousand years.

From Brahma's Kaya (body), a divine person emerged with a pen and ink in hand. Since he was born from the Kaya of Brahma, he was named "Kayastha," and because he was invisible in the mind of Brahma before birth, he was named "Chitragupta."

Brahma assigned him the duty of recording the deeds of all living beings in the world and assisting Yama, the God of Death, in dispensing justice. Lord Chitragupta is the progenitor of the Kayastha community, symbolizing intelligence, administrative prowess, and spiritual discipline.

During the Pooja, we recite this story to remind ourselves that our actions are being witnessed and that we must strive for a life of righteousness and learning.

auto_stories The Main Mantra

Masibhajansanyuktascharasi twam mahitale |
Lekhani-katini-hasta chitragupta namostute ||

"Salutations to Lord Chitragupta, who holds the pen and inkpot in his hands and records the history of the world."

Pooja Conclusion

Conclude the pooja by performing the Aarti with a camphor lamp or Ghee Diya. Distribute the Panchamrit and sweets as Prasad to all family members.
